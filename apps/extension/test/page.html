Kara extension debug

The same audio engine the offscreen document runs, fed from a local fixture instead of a stream the page reported. Synthesise one with node scripts/spike/make-fixtures.mjs; nothing is fetched from anywhere and nothing leaves this machine. The fixture's video plays untouched while the engine reads, decodes and separates the same track; once it is ready the video mutes and the stems play at its clock position. Switch the mode to hear the original sound again. The tone fixture's frequency is known, so the key can be measured off it.

What a Playwright run asserts on: __karaDebug.progress() never goes backwards and ends ready; __karaDebug.mediaSeconds() tracks the fixture's clock once the toggle is on, and a seek moves it to the new place; __karaDebug.dominantHz() before and after a transpose should differ by 2 ** (semitones / 12).

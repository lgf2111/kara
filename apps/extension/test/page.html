Kara extension debug

The same audio pipeline the offscreen document runs and the same delayed picture the content script draws, fed from a local fixture instead of from chrome.tabCapture. Synthesise one with node scripts/spike/make-fixtures.mjs; nothing is fetched and nothing leaves this machine. The fixture's audio plays undelayed, so the separated output arrives on top of it about eighteen seconds later — and the picture on screen is the overlay's, delayed to match, with the real video underneath it at opacity zero. Every fixture frame has its own timestamp burnt into it, so the delay can be read off the screen. While it gets ready the overlay holds one still frame with a progress bar on it, which is the same bar the panel and the popup draw, from the same report.

Pause and play go through the same table the content script reads, so they hold and resume rather than priming again: the delay stays put and __karaDebug.primes() does not move. A seek does move it. Add ?onReady=continue to watch the original play undelayed and then cross over.

The key buttons go through the same pitch shifter the extension runs, at the very end of the chain. __karaDebug.dominantHz() reads the loudest frequency coming out of the player off an analyser on its output, so a tone fixture is enough to prove the shift: the ratio of two readings either side of a transpose should be 2 ** (semitones / 12). Its latency does not change with the key, so pictureOffsetSeconds() should not move either.
